01 — About

I read the pricing pages so you don't have to.

StackWell started as a personal spreadsheet — a running list of every tool I tried while helping small businesses get their operations off the ground. It grew into something worth sharing.

Every recommendation here comes from actually using the product, reading the fine print on pricing tiers, and comparing it against the two or three tools people usually confuse it with. No tool makes the list just because it pays well.
